- **Output Interface:** BLE Bluetooth 5.0 + RS232 (Default)/RS485 + Analog (0~5V)
- **Voltage:** 9~36V DC
- **RS232 Communication:** 9600 baud rate (customized), 8 data bits, 1 stop bit, no flow control, no verification bit
- **Analog Output:** 0~5V
